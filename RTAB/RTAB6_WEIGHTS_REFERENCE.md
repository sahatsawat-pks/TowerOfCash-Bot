## Event Types (EventType.java)

**Events with official RTAB6 weights**

### Common Events (Weight 7)
- **Boost Charger** - Adds boost percentage
- **Double Deal** - Pick 2 spaces
- **Streak Bonus** - Reward based on streak

### Frequent Events (Weight 6)
- **Draw Two** - Draw 2 hidden commands
- **RtaB Market** - Shopping menu
- **Quad Damage** - 4x multiplier for one turn

### Uncommon Events (Weight 5)
- **Bowser** - Wheel of punishment
- **Peek Replenish** - Restore peek commands
- **Something For Everyone** - All players get something

### Notable Events (Weight 4)
- **Spoiler Tag (Hidden Commands)** - Weight varies by player count
  - 16-11 players: weight 1
  - 10-8 players: weight 2
  - 7-5 players: weight 3
  - 4 or less: weight 4
- **Joker** - Switch with another player
  - 2 players: weight 0 (disabled)
  - 3 players: weight 1
  - 4+ players: weight 4
- **One Buck Behind** - Copy leader's total -$1

### Rare Events (Weight 3)
- **Split & Share** - Pool and redistribute money
- **Boost Magnet** - Steal boosts from players
- **Draw Four** - Draw 4 hidden commands
  - 2 players: weight 0 (disabled)
  - 3 players: weight 1
  - 4+ players: weight 3

### Very Rare Events (Weight 2)
- **Minefield** - Many bombs placed
- **Lockdown** - All bombs become lockdown bombs
- **Final Countdown (End Round)** - Round ends after N turns

### Ultra Rare Events (Weight 1)
- **Super Joker** - Choose any player to swap with (disabled <4 players)
- **Starman** - Invincibility for several turns
- **Jackpot** - Massive cash prize

### Seasonal/Rotated Out (Weight 0)
- Lucky Space
- Times Ten (+1000% boost)
- Revival Chance
- Reverse
- Cursed Bomb
- Cash For All
- Minigames For All

## Notes

- **Grab Bag spaces** randomly contain cash, boosts, or minigames (not events) per RTAB6
- **Player count adjustments** make games more balanced:
  - Large games (8+): More cash, fewer minigames
  - Medium games (6-7): Slightly more cash, slightly fewer minigames
  - Small games (<4): More minigames, fewer events
- **2-player games** exclude dud bombs and certain events (Joker, Draw Four, Super Joker)
- **Seasonal events** have weight 0 and are only available during special periods
