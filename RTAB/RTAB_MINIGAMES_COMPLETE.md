#### 2. 👊 Punch a Bunch
- **Type:** Luck
- **Reward:** Up to $5,000,000
- **Weight:** 6
- **Description:** Pick 4 spaces from a 50-space board
- **How to Play:**
  - Board has 50 spaces
  - 5 spaces are BOMBS
  - 45 spaces contain cash ($50k - $5M)
  - Pick 4 times without hitting a bomb
  - After each pick, lowest cash value converts to bombs!
  - Can stop after any pick
  - Hit a bomb = lose everything

**Cash Distribution:**
- 5x Bombs (initially)
- 5x $50,000
- 5x $100,000
- 10x $200,000
- 10x $350,000
- 8x $500,000
- 4x $1,000,000
- 2x $2,000,000
- 1x $5,000,000

---

#### 3. 🎡 Bomb Roulette
- **Type:** Luck
- **Reward:** Up to $1,000,000+
- **Weight:** 5
- **Description:** Spin a 24-space wheel with cash, multipliers, and danger!
- **How to Play:**
  - 24-space wheel starts with:
    - 18 cash spaces ($10k - $750k)
    - 3 DOUBLE spaces (2x score)
    - 2 HALVE spaces (÷2 score)
    - 1 JOKER space (saves you from one bomb)
  - Each space you land on becomes a BOMB
  - Can stop after any spin
  - Hit a bomb without joker = lose everything

---

#### 4. 📈 Up and Down

- **Description:** Build a straight from 1-10!
- **How to Play:**
  - Pick cards trying to reveal 1, 2, 3... up to 10 in order
  - Each miss adds $50,000 to the pot
  - Complete the straight = win the pot + $1M bonus!
  - Too many misses = you lose

#### 6. ⚫ Double Zeroes
- **Type:** Luck
- **Reward:** Up to $50,000,000+
- **Weight:** 4
- **Description:** Build a number, then risk it for double zeros!
- **How to Play:**
  - 20 spaces: 9 Double Zeros, 10 digits (0-9), 1 Joker Zero

#### 40. 🎯 (O)ptimise
- **Type:** Skill
- **Reward:** $2,000,000
- **Weight:** 2
- **Description:** 3 color ladders! Strategic sphere picking!
- **How to Play:**
  - 3 colored money ladders (Red $500k, Green $1M, Blue $2M)
  - 10 tickets in barrel: 5 red, 3 green, 2 blue
  - 4 spheres (W, X, Y, Z) each contain number 1-4
  - Pick color + sphere each turn (9 turns total)
  - Sphere number > current target = ladder moves up
  - That number becomes new target
  - After each turn, 1 ticket torn up
  - At end, draw 1 remaining ticket = win that color's value!
  - Strategic: Give small targets to valuable colors
