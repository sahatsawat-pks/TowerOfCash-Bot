# 🎮 RTAB Season 6 - Complete Bombs, Commands & Events Implementation

## 💣 ALL BOMB TYPES (13 Total)

### Regular Bombs

#### 1. 💣 Normal Bomb (Weight: 26)
- **Effect:** Lose game, lose all money, lose items, reset multiplier
- **Penalty:** $50,000 stats penalty
- **Most common bomb type**

#### 2. 💰 Bankrupt Bomb (Weight: 3)
- **Effect:** Lose game AND all money, items, multiplier
- **Penalty:** Severe - bankrupt status
- **Takes everything you have**

#### 3. 💥 Cluster Bomb (Weight: 3)
- **Effect:** Explodes and places MORE bombs nearby
- **Penalty:** Creates 3-5 additional bombs in adjacent spaces
- **Chain reaction bomb!**

#### 4. 🤝 Collateral Damage Bomb (Weight: 3)
- **Effect:** You lose, but so do other players!
- **Penalty:** Damages all other living players
- **Revenge bomb - takes others down with you**

#### 5. 🔒 Loot Hold Bomb (Weight: 3)
- **Effect:** Lose game but KEEP your money!
- **Penalty:** Still lose items and multipliers
- **Least painful bomb**

#### 6. 🔄 Reverse Bomb (Weight: 3)
- **Effect:** Doesn't hurt picker, hurts PLACER!
- **Penalty:** Person who placed it loses instead
- **Ultimate revenge**

#### 7. 🚀 Boost Blast Bomb (Weight: 3)
- **Effect:** Lose game, lose money, BUT lose ALL your boost!
- **Penalty:** Boost multiplier reset to 100%
- **Targets your booster**

#### 8. 📊 Streak Blast Bomb (Weight: 3)
- **Effect:** Lose game, and your streak ends
- **Penalty:** Win/pick streak reset to 0
- **Kills momentum**

---

### Special Bombs

#### 9. ☠️ Loser Wheel Bomb (Weight: 1)
- **Effect:** Forces you to spin the Loser Wheel minigame!
- **Penalty:** Various penalties from wheel
- **Rare but unpredictable**

#### 10. 🎭 Dud Bomb (Weight: 1)
- **Effect:** Looks like a bomb but DOESN'T explode!
- **Penalty:** None - it's fake!
- **Free pass! (Not in 2P games)**
- **Shows as "BOMB" but safe**

#### 11. 🎯 Threshold Dud Bomb (Weight: 1)
- **Effect:** Fake bomb that ALSO gives you a threshold boost
- **Penalty:** None - bonus instead!
- **Secret blessing (Not in 2P games)**

---

### Event-Generated Bombs

#### 12. 😈 Cursed Bomb (Weight: 0 - Special)
- **Generated by:** Bowser Event
- **Effect:** Extremely dangerous, loses game plus extra penalties
- **Penalty:** Severe consequences

#### 13. 🔐 Lockdown Bomb (Weight: 0 - Special)
- **Generated by:** Lockdown Event
- **Effect:** Locks you out of turns
- **Penalty:** Skip turns, can't pick spaces

## 🎯 HIDDEN COMMANDS (11 Total)

### Common Commands (Weight: 11 each)

#### 1. 🛡️ FOLD (`!fold`)
- **Effect:** Drop out of round safely
- **Benefit:** Keep multipliers and minigames
- **Use:** Free escape from dangerous board
- **When:** Anytime during your turn

#### 2. ☠️ BLAMMO SUMMONER (`!blammo`)
- **Effect:** Give next player a BLAMMO!
- **Target:** Next player's turn
- **Use:** Force opponent to face instant bomb
- **When:** Anytime (affects NEXT turn)

#### 3. 🔀 SHUFFLER (`!shuffle 99`)
- **Effect:** Replace space contents with new random space
- **Target:** Any space (specify number)
- **Use:** Remove bombs from board
- **When:** Anytime before picking that space

#### 4. 💰 WAGERER (`!wager`)
- **Effect:** Force all players to add to prize pool
- **Amount:** Based on total banks
- **Use:** Create huge pot for winner
- **When:** Anytime

#### 5. 🎁 BONUS BAG (`!bonus [type]`)
- **Effect:** Skip turn, draw from bonus bag
- **Types:** cash, boost, game, event
- **Use:** Guaranteed reward instead of risking board
- **When:** Your turn (replaces normal pick)

#### 6. 👁️ EYE OF TRUTH (`!truth 99`)
- **Effect:** See EXACT contents of a space
- **Target:** Any space (specify number)
- **Use:** Perfect information about one space
- **When:** Before picking

#### 7. ❌ FAILSAFE (`!failsafe`)
- **Effect:** Instantly win if ALL spaces are bombs
- **Penalty:** -$1M if even ONE safe space exists
- **Use:** Emergency escape when board is 100% bombs
- **When:** When you think all remaining spaces are bombs

#### 8. 💣 MINESWEEPER (`!minesweeper 99`)
- **Effect:** Count bombs in 8 adjacent spaces
- **Target:** Any space (specify number)
- **Use:** Get bomb count around a space
- **When:** Anytime (helps plan safe route)

#### 9. 🛡️ BLAMMO REPELLENT (`!repel`)
- **Effect:** Block any player's BLAMMO
- **Alt Use:** Remove threshold situation
- **Use:** Save yourself or others from BLAMMO
- **When:** When BLAMMO is active OR threshold exists

---

### Ultra Rare Command

#### 10. 🌟 WILDCARD (Weight: 1)
- **Effect:** Can be used as ANY other hidden command!
- **Use:** One-time replacement for any command
- **When:** Anytime any other command would be valid
- **Ultra rare - most powerful command**
